# Appendix A - Details of Kell’s Physical Appearance (11·11·1126)  
## A.1. General Physique  
Kell possesses a fit physique, standing 184 cm tall and weighing 73 kg. Kell‘s skin is light beige in colour.  
  
## A.2. Hair  
Kell has straight, black hair measuring 45 cm in length.  
  
## A.3. Facial Features  
Kell’s facial features strongly resemble those of Yokose Rei, the female genetic donor from whom Kell’s genetic material was derived. Despite the strong resemblance between Kell’s and Yokose Rei’s facial features, Kell has brown irises and a straighter nasal bridge than her genetic donor.  
  
## A.4. Other Distinguishing Characteristics  
Kell possesses thermal pit organs beneath her eyes, the functions of which remain unknown.  
  
Kell’s upper arm and the lower and middle trapezius are covered in black-ink tattoos featuring patterns resembling an integrated circuit layout.
