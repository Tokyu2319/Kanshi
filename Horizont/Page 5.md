# Appendix - Details of Nelu Battel’s Attire (12·11·1126)  
## 1. General Description  
Nelu is dressed in a white cotton long-sleeved button-up shirt paired with field-grey wool-rayon pants, black belt, white cotton socks, field-grey wool-woven puttees, and ankle-high black leather dress boots. Nelu often has her sleeves rolled up.  
  
## 2. Undergarments  
Nelu wears a white cotton brassiere and white cotton panties.  
  
## 3. Accessories  
Nelu wears a military bag which contains her immediate essentials, medical items, tools, and utilities.
