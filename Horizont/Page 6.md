# 23·11·1126  
10:28 — Northernmost part of North Alkeluna  
Cloud Coverage: 2 oktas  
Wind: 2.1 m/s from 002º  
Kell alongside Ren, found a discarded black heavy wool greatcoat.  
  
Kell: <Hey! This’ll cover you up good.>  
  
Ren: <I guess... As long as it covers my butt from the wind, I’m happy.>  
  
Kell: <Cool.>  
  
Ren: <No, not cool. I need warmth.>  
  
Kell gives a light chuckle before undressing Ren of her oversized uniform to dress her in the greatcoat.  
  
Ren: \<It kinda stinks.>  
  
Kell: <We’ll wash it later, ‘kay?>  
  
Ren gives an affirmative murmur.  
  
10:30  
Kell and Ren left the abandoned farmhouse and headed further south.  
  
Ren: <You’d think we’d find people by now. I’m seeing no one.>  
  
Kell: <Maybe we’re… threading the needle?>  
  
Ren: <Maybe we are, because all I see here is ruin. Lift me up.>  
  
Kell: <Okay.>  
  
Kell picks up Ren while being careful about exposing her bare skin. Kell with Ren on her back jumps across a trench. After traversing over the trench, Ren forces herself off from Kell’s back.  
  
Ren: <Thanks, Kell.>  
  
Kell gives a smile.  
  
Kell: <Hehe. You’re welcome.>  
  
Kell and Ren continued south. Ren’s expression is sour.  
  
Kell: <Ren…>  
  
Ren: Hm?  
  
Kell: <You’ve been pouting since I got my clothes.>  
  
Ren: <Why wouldn’t I be? Everything we’ve found so far fits you.>  
  
Kell: <Oh don’t be like that. I’m sure we’ll find something that fits.>  
  
11:38  
Kell spotted a military vehicle.  
  
Kell: <Hey, look!>  
  
Kell picks up Ren on her shoulders.  
  
Ren: <Take us there. I’m tired of walking.>  
  
Kell: <Okay.>  
  
11:44  
Ren, perched on Kell’s shoulders, arrived at the military vehicle. The vehicle is a tank formerly operated by Dogelia’s armed forces. Kell and Ren stood in front of the vehicle for 3 seconds.  
  
Kell: <What is this?>  
  
Ren: <Dunno... Knock if there’s someone inside.>  
  
Kell: <Okay.>  
  
Kell gently knocks on the turret of the tank 3 times. No response can be heard inside. Kell looks at Ren.  
  
Ren: Hmm… <How about kicking it?>  
  
Kell: <Okay.>  
  
Kell kicks the tank, causing it to slide 20 cm from its initial position. The ground is muddy and wet from the snowmelt.  
  
Ren: <Oi! Not that much!>  
  
Kell gives a nervous smile.  
  
Kell: <Sorry…>  
  
Ren notices that the hatch of the tank is loose.  
  
Ren: <Hey, put me on top. I think I can get inside.>  
  
Kell: <Oh! Okay.>  
  
Kell places Ren on top of the turret of the tank, giving Ren access to the hatch. Ren lifts the hatch with effort.  
  
Ren: <Hah! Managed to get it open.>  
  
Kell: <What’re you about to do?>  
  
Ren: <I’m getting inside this thing. Kell, come in.>  
  
Kell: <Okay.>  
  
Ren gets inside the tank and makes room for Kell to get inside. Kell gets inside the tank but is struggling to find space.  
  
Kell: <It’s cramped…!>  
  
Ren: <Hah! I fit just fine, Kell.>  
  
Kell: <I thought we were finding clothes? What are we trying to get from this?>  
  
Ren finds the driver’s seat and crawls her way to sit on it.  
  
Ren: \<I think this thing can move.>  
  
Kell struggles to crawl to where Ren is sitting.  
  
Kell: Heh? [Deep breath] \<I wanna see it move.>  
  
11:49  
Ren examines the driver’s seat. Ren flips a switch, turns on the ignition, gives the throttle a light pump, and holds a button. Ren successfully starts the tank’s engine.  
  
Both: Woah!  
  
Kell: <How’d you do that?>  
  
Ren clicks her tongue 3 times.  
  
Ren: <I’m just good, is all.>  
  
Kell: <D-did you click your tongue, Ren? This thing’s noisy.>  
  
Ren flips another switch and revs  the engine.  
  
Kell: Woah! <Hey! We’re moving!>  
  
Ren: <Stick your head out, Kell! I can’t see anything here.>  
  
Kell: <On it!>  
  
Kell sticks her body out from the hatch of the tank.  
  
Kell: <The wind is so nice up here. Ren! The sun’s in front of us, we need to go right!>  
  
Ren: <Go right. Okay, Kell!>  
  
Ren steers the tank to head south.  
  
Kell: <Alright!>  
  
---  
  
From 15:57 to 17:01, Ren shuts down the tank’s engine and Kell goes outside the tank to forage for food. At 17:01, Kell returns to the tank with an assortment of wild nuts and fruits. Both begin eating, with Kell sharing most of her fruits with Ren. At 17:14, Ren starts the tank’s engine and heads further south.  
  
---  
  
18:00  
The tank runs out of fuel and stops. With the engine silent, the continuous sound of stridulation is heard.  
  
Kell: \<It stopped.>  
  
Ren: <Oh, I was hoping that wasn’t the fuel gauge. Turns out it is.>  
  
Kell: <It’s already sundown. I’m thinking we stay here.>  
  
Ren climbs out of the driver’s seat.  
  
Ren: <Do you think the animals here are nocturnal?>  
  
Kell: <What makes you say that?>  
  
Ren: <It’s because we haven’t seen anything except for birds. I want meat.>  
  
Kell: <Mangoes wasn’t enough for your big belly?>  
  
Ren makes a shocked expression.  
  
Ren: <Now what in the—! You can’t say that!>  
  
Kell: <W-what’s wrong with that?>  
  
Ren: <It’s true that I got more of your share, but you—calling out on it.>  
  
Kell: [Overlapping] <I’m not calling you fat or something. I’m saying—>  
  
Ren: <What do you mean I’ve got a big belly!?>  
  
Ren smiles and then giggles.  
  
Kell: <I’m saying—>  
  
Kell lets out an adorable laugh. Ren bursts into laughter.  
  
18:07  
Kell and Ren recovers from the moment of joy.  
  
Ren: <I’m gonna remember this.>  
  
---  
  
At 18:09, Kell and Ren began playing blindfold chess.[^1] Their game concluded at 22:19, with Ren emerging victorious. Afterward, Kell hugged Ren, and the two of them went to sleep.  
  
[^1]: **Blindfold chess** is a variation of chess where the players play a game without seeing or touching the physical pieces.
